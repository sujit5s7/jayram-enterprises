Which areas can be protected with invisible grills?+

Invisible grills are offered for balconies, windows, staircases and other open areas where safety is needed without blocking views, light or ventilation.

What types of safety nets are available?+

Jayaram’s current range includes balcony, children’s, duct-area, staircase, building, construction, industrial, swimming-pool, coconut-tree and cricket-practice safety nets.

Can you help with pigeon and bird problems?+

Yes. Pigeon nets, anti-bird nets, bird-protection nets and bird spikes are available for balconies, windows, terraces, ledges and other affected areas.
